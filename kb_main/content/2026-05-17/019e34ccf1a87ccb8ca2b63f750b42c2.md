## Findings

1. **MC-MLCC LocalCompressor E'=8 K=4 on this dataset = -0.0056 LB**. Bilibili paper prior (+0.0012) falsified.
2. **Peak valid 0.86332 was a NEW fork-series record but failed to transfer to LB**. This is the EXACT same failure mode as I-635 (multi-window item heat) — valid → LB is non-monotonic on this train/test split.
3. **The "valid ↑ / LB ↓" pattern now stands 11/11**. This is no longer a pattern — it is data-distribution-driven near-determinism. Future experiments should NOT use valid AUC as a stop signal; LB is the only signal.
4. **The architecture-axis is NOT proven closed** by this single KILL. The KILL is for ONE point in the space: K=4 + E'=8 + gamma=0 + injection at output_proj seam. Per the user's "降权 != 不相干" principle, the design family (block-wise low-rank cross interaction as residual head) has unexplored variants:
   - K ∈ {2, 8, 16} not tested
   - gamma init ∈ {0.1, learnable scale} not tested
   - Alternative injection points (between blocks, before output_proj) not tested
   - Combined with another axis (e.g., on top of cate-OOF heat I-643) may compensate
5. **However**: with 98258 (E'=16) eval still pending and likely KILL too (valid is +0.00076 higher than I-665 — under the "valid ↑ / LB ↓" rule, that mildly biases LB DOWN), the rank-ablation pair is on its way to confirming K=4 + the specific injection design is unlikely to transfer regardless of E'.
