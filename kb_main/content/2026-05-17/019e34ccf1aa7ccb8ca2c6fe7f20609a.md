## Findings

1. **The 4 verified DCN-family papers (DCN^2 / ML-DCN / DLF / Field-Aware Transformer) all converge on the same prior**: explicit field-aware cross-feature interactions outperform attention-only on anonymized industrial CTR data. This is the cleanest paper-grounded direction available given all constraints.
2. **ML-DCN (Pinterest 2026)** is the most directly transferable — explicitly latency-aware, masked low-rank cross, "neutral serving cost". This matches our latency budget constraint precisely.
