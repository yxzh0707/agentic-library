## Notes

The flat schema is anonymized and does not label sequence semantic fields as
`item_id`. Existing EDA identifies the strongest item-like sideinfo columns as
`domain_a_seq_38`, `domain_b_seq_69`, `domain_c_seq_47`, and `domain_d_seq_23`;
dataset sideinfo slots are therefore `seq_a:0`, `seq_b:1`, `seq_c:10`,
`seq_d:6`. This is the only non-structural assumption in I-638.
