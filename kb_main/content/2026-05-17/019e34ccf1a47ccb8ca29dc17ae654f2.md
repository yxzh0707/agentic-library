# External Research — Item-ID OOV Architectural Solutions

## Search Angles (used)

- `LLM / text-encoder as item backbone in CTR/recommendation/ranking` ; ≥2025 ; industrial (Meta/Alibaba/Kuaishou/ByteDance/Tencent/Baidu) papers substituting item-id embedding with text/content tower

## Verified Findings

### 1. MOON Embedding: Multimodal Representation Learning for E-commerce Search Advertising

- **Authors**: Chenghan Fu, Daoze Zhang, Yukang Lin, et al.
- **Year / venue**: 2025, arXiv preprint
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11305
- **Verification**: PASS — fetched title matches; year ≥ floor (2025 ≥ 2025).
- **Finding**: Alibaba/Alimama deploys a multimodal product/query representation system across Taobao search advertising retrieval, relevance, and ranking. Core message: learned multimodal product representations from text/image/content signals — optimized through pretraining → post-training → downstream — outperform pure sparse-ID approaches.
- **Relevance to UniRec-KDDCup2026 pCVR**: Direct architectural recipe for replacing `item_id_hash` (1M sparse, KILLed in I-608) and `item_heat` (15-dim dense, KILLed in I-635) with an offline product/ad content embedding table. Pattern: content embedding becomes the stable backbone; ID/heat features become optional residual features only when seen at inference time.

### 2. MIM: Multi-modal Content Interest Modeling Paradigm for User Behavior Modeling

- **Authors**: Bencheng Yan, Si Chen, Shichang Jia, et al.
- **Year / venue**: 2025, arXiv preprint
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.00321
- **Verification**: PASS — fetched title matches; year ≥ floor.
- **Finding**: Alibaba paper explicitly argues that ID embeddings fail to capture real content preferences and break down in cold-start and long-tail scenarios. Approach: adapt foundation/multimodal models to domain data, align content embeddings with user-interest signals, validated by online Taobao CTR + RPM gains.
- **Relevance to UniRec-KDDCup2026 pCVR**: Encode item title/description/category/creative-text into dense vectors → fine-tune or align them with click/conversion labels → feed the aligned content vector into the pCVR ranker INSTEAD of `item_id_hash`. The same text tower can score unseen items at inference without a learned item-id embedding — directly applicable to the train/test OOV split that has killed every item-id-dependent feature so far.

### 3. FilterLLM: Text-To-Distribution LLM for Billion-Scale Cold-Start Recommendation

- **Authors**: Ruochen Liu, Hao Chen, Yuanchen Bei, et al.
- **Year / venue**: 2025, arXiv preprint
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16924
- **Verification**: PASS — fetched title matches; year ≥ floor.
- **Finding**: Alibaba framework that maps item text/content into a predicted interaction distribution over users, avoiding pairwise LLM scoring for every user-item pair. Deployed at Alibaba billion-scale with large efficiency gains and online A/B validation for cold-item recommendation.
- **Relevance to UniRec-KDDCup2026 pCVR**: The "text-to-distribution" idea adapts into a lightweight content prior — generate a dense cold-item representation or user-segment affinity vector from item text, concatenate into the CVR model. Targets exactly the unseen-`item_id` case where `item_heat` is meaningless or misleading.

### 4. QARM V2: Quantitative Alignment Multi-Modal Recommendation for Reasoning User Sequence Modeling

- **Authors**: Tian Xia, Jiaqi Zhang, Yueyang Liu, et al.
- **Year / venue**: 2026, arXiv preprint
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08559
- **Verification**: PASS — fetched title matches; year ≥ floor (newest of the batch).
- **Finding**: Kuaishou bridges LLM semantic understanding and industrial RecSys objectives, explicitly motivated by the weak generalization and low information density of ID-based embeddings in sequence modeling. Approach: align LLM-derived semantic representations with business recommendation objectives (not raw frozen content vectors).
- **Relevance to UniRec-KDDCup2026 pCVR**: Convert item text/content embeddings into aligned semantic codes or dense item vectors trained against conversion/click objectives, then use those in place of the 1M sparse `item_id_hash`. For the OOV-heavy split: unseen items still map through the same content encoder/alignment layer — no training-time ID lookup required.

### 5. OneRec-Think: In-Text Reasoning for Generative Recommendation

- **Authors**: Zhanyu Liu, Shiyao Wang, Xingmei Wang, et al.
- **Year / venue**: 2025, arXiv preprint
- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11639
- **Verification**: PASS — fetched title matches; year ≥ floor.
- **Finding**: Kuaishou LLM-based generative recommendation using item-textual alignment for semantic grounding, reasoning activation, and recommendation-specific reward optimization. Reports public-benchmark gains + industrial Kuaishou deployment with an online APP Stay Time lift.
- **Relevance to UniRec-KDDCup2026 pCVR**: Map each ad/item into text-aligned semantic IDs or dense vectors, train the CVR model over those representations instead of raw item IDs. The item representation derives from content, not from whether the exact `item_id` appeared in training.

## Notable Bias in the Result Set

All 5 papers come from Chinese tech giants (Alibaba × 3, Kuaishou × 2). 0 from Meta / ByteDance / Baidu / Tencent / industry research labs in the US. Two interpretations:

1. **Confirmation**: Chinese e-commerce/short-video platforms have the largest cold-item problems, so they publish the most on this. Result set is representative.
2. **Survivor bias**: arXiv search bias toward Chinese groups that post English preprints; Tencent's own pCVR work (the host of this competition!) may not have public arxiv counterparts.

Action: if the user wants Meta/Tencent-specific work, that's a follow-up search round. Not in scope of this single-angle test.
