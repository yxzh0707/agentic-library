## Notes

Only 12 new parameters (per-domain alpha/temp/bias × 4 domains). No sparse
embeddings added — stays in the dense projection family that I-605 validated.

The gate operates on the output of `seq_time_proj` (post Linear projection),
not on raw embeddings. This means it modulates the time-enriched token
representations by their relevance to the target item.
