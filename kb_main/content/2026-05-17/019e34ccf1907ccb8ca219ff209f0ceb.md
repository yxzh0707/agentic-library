# I-674 — Ado ue/stat Dense Split + Int-Pair Gate

Forks `experiments/I-670_mldcn_stable_cross/code/` (LB SOTA 0.825484) and keeps the I-670 ML-DCN stable cross branch.

## Design

- Splits `user_dense` into `ue_dense = user_dense_feats_61 || user_dense_feats_87` with shape `(B, 576)`.
- Builds `stat_dense = log1p(user_dense_feats_62..66)` with shape `(B, 17)`.
- Drops `user_dense_feats_89/90/91` from dataset read/storage.
- Adds `AdoDensePaths`:
  - `ue_dense -> ue_emb` and `stat_dense -> stat_emb`, both `(B, d_model)`.
  - Uses `ue_emb + stat_emb` to form a per-user-int-token gate.
  - Applies a zero-init residual `gamma_dense` so step-0 behavior remains close to I-670.
- Concatenates `ue_emb` and `stat_emb` as additional non-sequential tokens.
- Uses the gated user-int NS tokens in place of standard user-int NS tokens.
- Monitors validation logloss with `EarlyStopping(direction='min')`.

## Safety Constraints

- Does not add or modify `user_id` / `item_id` learned embeddings.
- Keeps the ML-DCN cross branch in place.
- Initializes the new residual gate to zero to preserve baseline behavior at step 0.
