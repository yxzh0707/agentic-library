## Outcome: KILL deep

Per-time-bucket learnable scalar weight on seq tokens. Identity init
(weights=1.0) preserved I-643 baseline at step 0, so gradient signal
drove weights AWAY from baseline during training — and they drifted in
the wrong direction by ~15.5sigma. Codex round-4 was wrong direction.

The hypothesis was D0->D9 4x CTR drift would push weights to favor
recent buckets. Reality: the learned bucket weights overfit train-period
correlations and don't transfer.

Confirms gating-axis closure extends to time-bucket gating, not just
target-item or feat-channel gating. We thought time-bucket gating was
safe because it doesn't bind to item_id. Wrong — any learnable
multiplicative scaling on seq tokens is non-transferable on this data.
