## Verified Papers

### 1. Federated Vision Transformer with Adaptive Focal Loss for Medical Image Classification
- arXiv ID: 2602.01633v1
- Family: Adaptive Focal / client-distribution-aware class-balanced focal
- Mechanism (1 line): Dynamic adaptive focal loss changes the class-imbalance coefficient per client from local sample/class distributions, paired with client-aware aggregation.
- Distinguishing claim vs standard focal: Standard focal uses fixed alpha/gamma; DAFL explicitly conditions the loss weights on local distribution heterogeneity, so it is the only screened 2026 focal evolution with a plausible distribution-shift hook.
- "Unless" category satisfied: distribution-shift-aware (weak; FL client heterogeneity, not train/test CTR drift)
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: none; medical image datasets only, with accuracy improvements reported versus DenseNet/ResNet/ViT/FedCLIP/Swin/CoAtNet/MixNet, not AUC on CTR/CVR.
- PCVR fit: ⚠️ only if reframed as time-slice/domain-aware alpha(t/domain); no evidence on sparse recommender IDs or delayed conversion labels.
- Implementation cost: ~40-80 lines for per-slice positive-rate alpha and optional gamma schedule; no backbone change.
- 2026 evidence: arXiv submitted 2026-02-02; accepted in Knowledge-Based Systems.

### 2. 3D Fourier-based Global Feature Extraction for Hyperspectral Image Classification
- arXiv ID: 2603.16426v1
- Family: Adaptive Focal / class-wise dynamic focal
- Mechanism (1 line): Adaptive focal loss dynamically adjusts class-wise focusing and weighting to improve minority-class discrimination in hyperspectral image classification.
- Distinguishing claim vs standard focal: Uses class-wise adaptive focusing/weights rather than one global alpha/gamma, but the abstract frames it as class-imbalance handling only.
- "Unless" category satisfied: none; does not claim calibration, train/test shift robustness, or label-noise correction.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: none; no CTR/CVR evaluation.
- PCVR fit: ❌ reject for now; it reopens the killed imbalance-only focal axis without the required unless mechanism.
- Implementation cost: ~30-60 lines, but not recommended.
- 2026 evidence: arXiv submitted 2026-03-17.

### 3. Functional Properties of the Focal-Entropy
- arXiv ID: 2603.02533v1
- Family: Focal-loss theory / calibration screen
- Mechanism (1 line): Studies focal-entropy as the distributional analogue of focal loss and characterizes its minimizer under imbalance.
- Distinguishing claim vs standard focal: Negative for our use case: the minimizer can depart substantially from the data distribution, with mid-probability amplification and over-suppression of tiny probabilities under extreme imbalance.
- "Unless" category satisfied: calibration (negative evidence; suggests focal does not preserve posterior/Platt-friendly probabilities by default).
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: none; theoretical + validation experiments, no recommender AUC.
- PCVR fit: ❌ do not implement; use as evidence that focal can damage calibrated CVR probabilities under severe imbalance/drift.
- Implementation cost: 0 lines; informs loss-axis closure.
- 2026 evidence: arXiv submitted 2026-03-03; accepted to AISTATS 2026.

### 4. Differential Attention-Augmented BiomedCLIP with Asymmetric Focal Optimization for Imbalanced Multi-Label Video Capsule Endoscopy Classification
- arXiv ID: 2603.17879v1
- Family: Asymmetric Focal
- Mechanism (1 line): Combines asymmetric focal loss with weighted sampling, mixup, per-class thresholds, and temporal post-processing for extremely rare multi-label medical events.
- Distinguishing claim vs standard focal: Positive/negative class focusing differs, but it is bundled with sampler/threshold/post-processing and not isolated as a robust focal mechanism.
- "Unless" category satisfied: none; imbalance-only, no calibration/shift/noisy-label argument.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: none; reports temporal mAP on RARE-VISION test set, not CTR/CVR AUC.
- PCVR fit: ❌ reject; asymmetric focal is still focal tuning on imbalance and conflicts with our killed alpha sweep unless paired with a new mechanism.
- Implementation cost: ~30-50 lines, not recommended.
- 2026 evidence: arXiv submitted 2026-03-18; ICPR 2026 RARE-VISION Competition paper.

### 5. Predicting Upcoming Stuttering Events from Three-Second Audio: Stratified Evaluation Reveals Severity-Selective Precursors, and the Model Deploys Fully On-Device
- arXiv ID: 2604.27279v1
- Family: Asymmetric Focal ablation / calibrated-output negative control
- Mechanism (1 line): Tests asymmetric focal loss as one of five negative ablations; final model relies on vanilla baseline plus Platt-calibrated outputs.
- Distinguishing claim vs standard focal: Direct negative ablation: asymmetric focal did not improve the baseline, while Platt calibration sharply reduced ECE.
- "Unless" category satisfied: calibration (negative for focal; supports calibrating BCE outputs rather than focalizing the loss).
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: none; audio AUC only, no CTR/CVR.
- PCVR fit: ❌ do not implement; useful as corroboration that calibration should be post-hoc/stacked, not focal-driven.
- Implementation cost: 0 lines for focal; calibration stack already in-flight as I-672.
- 2026 evidence: arXiv submitted 2026-04-30.

### 6. Generative Cross-Entropy: A Strictly Proper Loss for Data-Efficient Classification
- arXiv ID: 2604.06689v2
- Family: Calibration/OOD alternative, not focal
- Mechanism (1 line): Replaces CE with a batch-coupled strictly proper loss whose population optimum is the true posterior under stated conditions.
- Distinguishing claim vs standard focal: This is the opposite direction from focal: preserve proper scoring/calibration while improving small-data and imbalanced regimes.
- "Unless" category satisfied: calibration and distribution-shift/OOD detection, but not a focal variant.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: none; classification datasets only, no recommender AUC.
- PCVR fit: ⚠️ candidate for later regularization/calibration axis, not for focal-axis resurrection; batch-coupled loss may be risky with temporal sampling.
- Implementation cost: ~80-150 lines if adapted to binary BCE logits with batch class coupling.
- 2026 evidence: arXiv submitted 2026-04-08; revised 2026-05-09.

## Synthesis

Top candidates by P(transfers in our 4x CTR drift regime) x cost-efficiency:

1. **DAFL-style domain/time-slice adaptive alpha** from arXiv:2602.01633 — **weak maybe**. It is the only found 2026 focal evolution with an explicit distribution-heterogeneity mechanism. However, evidence is medical FL accuracy, not CTR/CVR AUC, and the mechanism is still mostly class-imbalance weighting. If tested, constrain to a tiny offline-only ablation: compute alpha per temporal block/domain bucket and keep gamma conservative. Do not spend LB quota unless offline AUC/logloss/calibration improves by >0.0005 and ECE does not regress.
2. **GenCE / proper-loss direction** from arXiv:2604.06689 — **not focal, but more aligned with the unless clause**. If the objective is calibration under drift, a strictly proper CE evolution is more compatible with PCVR probabilities than focal. This belongs to the regularization/calibration axis, not the focal axis.

**Bottom line:** no 2026 paper found that cleanly satisfies all constraints: focal evolution + 2026 arXiv prefix >=2601 + calibration/distribution-shift/noisy-label mechanism + CTR/CVR evidence. The forum's "focal loss" claim does not survive this 2026 scrutiny as a reason to reopen standard focal. Recommend keeping the loss-axis CLOSED, with only a low-cost offline DAFL-style temporal/domain adaptive-alpha probe allowed if we need to explicitly falsify the remaining focal variant.
