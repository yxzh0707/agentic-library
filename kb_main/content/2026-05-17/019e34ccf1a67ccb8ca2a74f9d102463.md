## Findings

1. **The OOV problem the project has been hitting (I-608, I-635) is a recognized open problem in 2025-2026 industrial CTR research, with concrete architectural solutions published**. The common pattern across all 5 papers: **replace the learned ID embedding with a content-derived dense representation** (text encoder, multimodal embedder, or aligned semantic codes).
2. **Three converging recipes from Alibaba** (MOON, MIM, FilterLLM) suggest the problem has reached "best-practice" status, not "frontier" status. This is reassuring — the solution doesn't require frontier-paper risk.
3. **Kuaishou's QARM V2 (2026) is the freshest signal** — it specifically attacks "weak generalization and low information density of ID-based embeddings", which is the exact UniRec-KDDCup2026 pain. Pattern: align content embeddings with the downstream conversion objective rather than using raw frozen content vectors as untrained side features.
4. **Bias note**: All 5 papers come from Chinese tech giants. 0 from Meta/Tencent/Baidu/etc. May reflect arxiv-availability bias more than reality. Follow-up search needed if Meta/Tencent-specific work is required.
