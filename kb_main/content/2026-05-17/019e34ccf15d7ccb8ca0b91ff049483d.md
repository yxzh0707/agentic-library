## Outcome

Negative result, **intentional**. Delta vs baseline-0 = -0.01376 leaderboard.

Confirms the hypothesis raised by Codex during I-502 review: cold restart is not optional defensive code, it is the primary regularizer that allows the 240M-param PCVRHyFormer to converge over multiple epochs on TencentGR. Without it, the model burns through its capacity memorizing per-row IDs in epoch 1 and over-fits the training time-window from epoch 2 onward.

Memory rule sunk: `feedback_cold_restart_load_bearing.md` — never disable in future runs.
