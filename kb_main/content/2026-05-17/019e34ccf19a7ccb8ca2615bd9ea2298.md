### 1. Contrastive Learning for Diversity-Aware Product Recommendations in Retail
- arXiv ID: 2602.08886
- Mechanism family: Contrastive
- Mechanism (1 line): Adds contrastive learning with carefully selected negative samples to improve long-tail catalog exposure without replacing the serving recommender pipeline.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract reports offline and online gains in catalog coverage while preserving recommendation quality.
- PCVR fit: ✅ + ID-only retail recommendation and negative-sample contrast can train on anonymized integer IDs; no text/image/url dependency and could regularize 4x drift without changing PCVRHyFormer + RankMixer inference.
- Implementation cost: ~120 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: Uses contrastive negative selection for diversity/long-tail robustness, not focal loss, pairwise ranking, id_dropout, target-aware gates, Fourier time features, or an explicit cross-branch architecture.

### 2. Meta-Learning and Targeted Differential Privacy to Improve the Accuracy-Privacy Trade-off in Recommendations
- arXiv ID: 2604.26390
- Mechanism family: Meta
- Mechanism (1 line): Uses model-level meta-learning to make recommender training robust to privacy-induced perturbations, paired with selective DP on sensitive-looking user data.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract reports better recommendation accuracy/privacy trade-off than uniform DP and full-DP baselines.
- PCVR fit: ⚠️ + meta-learning for noise/distribution adaptation is relevant to 5-day 4x CTR drift on anonymized IDs, but targeted DP depends on sensitive attributes such as age/gender that may not exist in the 120 flat columns.
- Implementation cost: ~220 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: The transferable mechanism is a meta-objective for robustness under perturbed distributions, not AdamW beta2 tuning, SAM, Platt/MonoCal calibration, recency weighting, or new item embeddings.

### 3. Enhancing Bandit Algorithms with LLMs for Time-varying User Preferences in Streaming Recommendations
- arXiv ID: 2602.08067
- Mechanism family: Hypernetwork
- Mechanism (1 line): HyperBandit+ uses a time-aware hypernetwork that consumes time features and generates reward-estimator parameters for evolving user preferences.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract claims improved streaming recommendation performance through time-aware adaptation and LLM warm start.
- PCVR fit: ⚠️ + the hypernetwork conditioning can be a drop-in adapter for 4x day-level CTR drift, but the LLM warm-start portion is not usable with anonymized integer IDs and should be omitted.
- Implementation cost: ~180 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: Parameter generation from time context is distinct from time-feature pre-projection gating, target-aware token gates, dense Fourier time axes, seq-time Fourier+recency, and FiLM-Gated-AttnBias.

### 4. Robust Aggregation for Federated Sequential Recommendation with Sparse and Poisoned Data
- arXiv ID: 2602.23982
- Mechanism family: Adversarial
- Mechanism (1 line): Introduces defence-aware aggregation that identifies/down-weights unreliable or poisoned updates and adds sequence-aware regularisation for temporal coherence.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract reports robustness under sparse and adversarial sequential-recommendation conditions.
- PCVR fit: ⚠️ + no inference-time domain labels are required, and training-only robust down-weighting could be adapted to drifted day/client shards, but the federated setting does not match the centralized 907K/310K PCVR data.
- Implementation cost: ~260 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: Focuses on adversarial/poisoned training-signal robustness and temporal coherence, not pairwise ranking, focal loss, id embedding expansion, SAM, or promoted recency/Fourier weighting.

### 5. FuXi-Linear: Unleashing the Power of Linear Attention in Long-term Time-aware Sequential Recommendation
- arXiv ID: 2602.23671
- Mechanism family: Attention
- Mechanism (1 line): Replaces quadratic sequence attention with linear-complexity channels that separate temporal-retention and positional information for long time-aware sequences.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract claims efficient long-sequence recommendation and addresses overlooked temporal/positional signals.
- PCVR fit: ✅ + PCVRHyFormer sequence lengths of 256-512 could benefit from linear attention; uses behavioral/time IDs rather than text/image and can be tested as an internal attention block while keeping RankMixer.
- Implementation cost: ~240 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: The core is a linear attention variant with separated temporal and positional channels, not d_model-interior multi-head MC-MLCC, dense Fourier, seq-time Fourier+recency, or time-feature pre-projection gating.

### 6. Jointly Optimizing Debiased CTR and Uplift for Coupons Marketing: A Unified Causal Framework
- arXiv ID: 2602.12972
- Mechanism family: Calibration
- Mechanism (1 line): UniMVT disentangles confounding from treatment-sensitive representations to reconstruct debiased base CTR and multi-valued intensity-response curves.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract targets miscalibrated base CTR caused by coupon interventions and reports improved debiased CTR/uplift optimization.
- PCVR fit: ⚠️ + useful as calibration beyond Platt when 4x CTR drift reflects hidden interventions or day effects, but PCVR data may lack explicit coupon/treatment intensity fields among the anonymized columns.
- Implementation cost: ~200 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: Counterfactual debiasing and treatment-intensity calibration differ from MLPlatt/MonoCal heads, Platt scaling, cate-OOF heat statistics, focal loss, and pairwise ranking.

### 7. Mixture of Sequence: Theme-Aware Mixture-of-Experts for Long-Sequence Recommendation
- arXiv ID: 2604.20858
- Mechanism family: MoE
- Mechanism (1 line): MoS routes long user sequences into latent theme-specific, multi-scale subsequences to handle session hopping and interest shifts.
- Reported lift on CTR/CVR: Not quantified in abstract; abstract claims more accurate click-through prediction from noisy long sequences with theme-aware routing.
- PCVR fit: ✅ + latent routing from behavior sequences works with anonymized integer IDs, directly targets interest shifts over time, and can wrap PCVRHyFormer features without inference-time domain labels.
- Implementation cost: ~220 lines
- Why novel relative to closed/promoted axes: Theme-aware MoE over subsequences is different from explicit cross-branch architecture, MGDIN grouped cross, target-aware token gates, recency weighting, or new item-id embedding tricks.

## Synthesis
Top EV candidates are FuXi-Linear, Mixture of Sequence, and the IKEA contrastive negative-sampling paper. FuXi-Linear has the best EV x P(success) / cost because replacing or augmenting only the 256-512-length sequence attention path attacks a known HyFormer bottleneck while preserving anonymized-ID compatibility. MoS is second: theme-aware subsequence routing is well aligned with 5-day 4x CTR drift and user-interest hopping, but MoE routing adds more integration risk. Contrastive diversity training is the cheapest and safest regularizer; expected lift may be smaller, yet it is a clean training-only addition that avoids all closed/promoted axes and could plausibly clear the +0.0005 AUC bar as an ensemble/regularization improvement.
