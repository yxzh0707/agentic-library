# TAAC x KDD Cup 2026 — 赛题原文

> 以下内容摘录自官方网站 https://algo.qq.com/ ，仅供参考，最终以官方发布为准。

## 1. Technical Background / 赛题背景

> Recommender systems power both large-scale content platforms (feeds, short videos, etc.) and digital advertising (CTR/CVR prediction, etc.), directly shaping user experience, engagement, and revenue. Operating under massive traffic and strict latency constraints, these systems make billions of real-time decisions daily and underpin a hundred-billion dollar digital advertising market. Over the past two decades, recommendation research has progressed along two major branches: **feature interaction models**, which focus on modeling high-dimensional multi-field categorical and contextual features, and **sequential models**, which capture the temporal dynamics of user behavior through embedding-based retrieval systems and Transformer-style ranking models. While both paradigms have achieved significant success, they have largely evolved independently. This separation has created structural bottlenecks in industrial systems: shallow cross-paradigm interaction, inconsistent optimization objectives, limited scalability, and increasing hardware and engineering complexity. As sequence lengths and model scales continue to grow, these fragmented architectures become increasingly inefficient.
>
> The KDD Cup 2026 Tencent UNI-REC Challenge focuses on unifying two traditionally separate branches in large-scale recommendation/advertising ranking—**sequential behavior modeling** and **non-sequential multi-field feature interaction**. Participants are encouraged to build a **single, homogeneous backbone** that handles both types of features and models their correlations with **stackable, unified blocks.** The competition will release a privacy-preserving, de-identified industrial-scale dataset and provide an off-the-shelf baseline/starter kit plus organizer-provisioned training/evaluation resources via the **AngelML** platform.

**已提及的特征交互模型：** Wide & Deep, FM, CF, FFM, FwFM, FmFM, NFM, AFM, DCN, DCNV2, xDeepFM, AutoInt, FinalMLP, RankMixer, Wukong, DHEN, Multi-Embedding, FeatureGeneration, HiFormer, HyFormer, OneTrans, InterFormer, HeMix, Kunlun, MixFormer, MtMixAtt, HHFT

**已提及的序列模型：** DIN, DIEN, DSIN, TIN, SIM, TWIN, TWINV2, DARE, Longer, SASRec, BST, BERT4Rec, GRU4Rec, HSTU, TransAct

## 2. Competition Task / 赛题描述

> The main objective of this competition is to design a unified, stackable modeling block that simultaneously handles multi-field non-sequential tokens and sequential behavior tokens in one architecture.
>
> Participants will be given the dataset described above, where each training/test instance corresponds to a triplet (user, context, target ad/item). Each instance consists of (1) sequential behavior data, i.e., chronological user interaction histories with items and timestamps across multiple users, and (2) non-sequential multi-field features grouped into four categories: User Features, Ads Features, Context Features (sparse), and Cross Features (dense embeddings), all subject to privacy protection.
>
> Non-sequential multi-field features (user, ad, context, and cross features) and a user behavior sequence with heterogeneous side information. The participants should build an effective model to capture the correlations between all these features, which outputs a predicted conversion rate (pCVR).
>
> Participants are encouraged to develop a **unified tokenization scheme** and a **homogeneous, stackable backbone** that jointly models sequential user behaviors and non-sequential multi-field features within a single architecture for CVR prediction. Submissions will be ranked by a single AUC of ROC metric. Beyond the leaderboard, we will offer two innovation awards—**Unified Block Innovation Award ($45,000)** and **Scaling Law Innovation Award ($45,000)**—to recognize outstanding advances in unified architectures and systematic scaling-law exploration, respectively. These awards are independent of leaderboard rank, and the workshop paper review will emphasize novelty and insight along these two directions rather than focusing solely on AUC.
>
> The model is trained via loop-based optimization with a cross entropy loss. The final leaderboard score is the AUC of ROC score.

## 3. Dataset Description / 数据描述

> The dataset released in this competition is fully anonymized and does not reflect the exact production characteristics of Tencent's advertising platform.
>
> Our dataset is a large-scale industrial dataset constructed from real-world advertising logs. It consists of two main components: (1) user behavior sequences and (2) non-sequential multi-field features.
>
> User behavior sequences contain interaction events between users and items (e.g., exposure, click, conversion), each associated with side information such as timestamps and action types. Multi-field features include user attributes, item attributes, contextual signals, and cross features.
>
> To ensure fairness and protect privacy, all sparse features are represented as anonymized integer IDs, and dense features are provided as fixed-length float vectors. No raw content (e.g., text, image, URL) or personally identifiable information is released.

**Sequential Data（用户行为序列）：**


## 4. Evaluation Metrics / 评价指标

> We will rank all teams using a **single AUC of ROC** metric (higher is better). To ensure practicality, each submission must also satisfy a **track- and round-specific inference latency limit** under the official evaluation environment and protocol; submissions that exceed the latency budget will be **invalid** and therefore not ranked, regardless of AUC.
>
> Submissions with **ROC-AUC <= 0.50** will not be displayed on the leaderboard in either round.
>
> If two or more teams achieve identical ROC-AUC on the relevant leaderboard, ties will be broken by (a) lower measured inference latency under the official measurement methodology, and then, if necessary, (b) earlier submission timestamp, or another deterministic rule published by the Sponsor on the official platform. No random or chance-based method will be used to break ties.

---

## 5. Submission Requirements / 提交要求

> All submissions must comply with the official submission interface and required formats published on the competition platform. Depending on the round and track, submissions may be required to take the form of batch prediction outputs and/or a containerized inference service or pipeline that implements the specified input/output protocol.
>
> To emphasize methodological clarity and enable fair comparison, we prohibit model ensembling throughout the competition.
>
> After the round 2 finishes, each team must submit (a) complete end-to-end training and inference code, and (b) a paper to the KDD Cup workshop describing the team's solution.
