## Surprises that changed strategy

1. **I-605 +0.006 with only +4352 dense params** — vastly more efficient than I-608's +67M sparse params for -0.0003.
2. **Dense projection on seq-time signal beats sparse high-card token** for TencentGR — possibly because dense projections enforce smoothness across train/test distribution shift, while sparse embeddings can only memorize.
3. **I-606 -0.003 was a fair trade for 1.87x speed** — not just statement of faith; I-605 needed I-606 base to actually run within budget.
